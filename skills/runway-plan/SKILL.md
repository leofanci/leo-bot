# Runway Plan

Not financial, tax or legal advice. Plain cash math to decide with. Taxes, payroll
and loans: confirm with an accountant.

## Read first
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md` (stage, revenue, team) and any unit
  economics page.
- The owner's numbers: cash in the bank, monthly revenue, monthly costs by line.
  Ask for them once if missing; never guess the cash balance.

## Method
1. **List monthly costs by line:** people (including what the owner pays themself),
   tools, hosting, rent, marketing, other. Mark fixed and variable.
2. **Monthly revenue:** what is actually collected, not booked.
3. **Net burn** = costs - revenue. **Runway months** = cash / net burn.
4. **Three scenarios for 12 months:**
   - Base: current trend.
   - Low: revenue flat or down 20%, one surprise cost.
   - High: the growth the evidence supports, not the hoped one.
   Give runway and the month cash runs out in each.
5. **The decision date:** the month by which the owner must have raised money,
   cut costs or reached break-even, with 3 months of margin.
6. **Moves to extend runway**, each with months gained: cut a tool, pause a cost,
   annual prepay offers, a price change, a paid pilot.

## Options
Two or three plans: cut to extend, invest to grow faster, or hold and watch a
trigger. For each, runway after the plan and the main risk.

## Recommendation
One plan, the trigger that would make the owner switch plans, and the next number to
check each month.

## Before you reply
- Cash and costs are the owner's numbers; anything assumed is marked.
- Scenarios use stated assumptions, not optimism.
- Nothing is paid, moved or cancelled: those are the owner's actions.
