# Deal Review

## Read first
- The workspace's deals list (`get-list`) or the deals the owner pastes.
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md` (offer, price) and the pricing memo.
- The evidence log for objections already heard.
- No list: ask for the open deals as lines (who, stage, last contact, value).

## Method
1. **Clean the stages.** A deal sits at the stage the buyer has reached, not the one
   hoped for: contacted, talked, problem confirmed, proposal sent, verbal yes, won.
2. **Flag each deal:**
   - Stale: no contact in 14 days (or the owner's number).
   - No next step: nothing agreed with a date.
   - Single-threaded: only one person on their side knows.
   - Missing a fact: budget, decision maker or timing unknown past the talked stage.
3. **Find the pattern:** where most deals stop, and the objection heard most.
4. **One next step per live deal**, concrete and dated: a question to ask, a proof
   to send, a call with the decision maker. Messages are drafts.
5. **Call the dead ones:** suggest closing deals with no reply after 3 tries, so
   the pipeline stays honest.

## Options
When the pattern points to a fix, give two or three: change the offer (a pilot, a
lower first step), change the qualification (drop a segment), or change the follow-up
rhythm. One line on the trade-off of each.

## Recommendation
The three deals to work this week, the one fix for the pattern, and the expected
value of the pipeline counting only deals past "problem confirmed".

## Before you reply
- Stages come from facts heard, not hopes.
- Every live deal has one dated next step.
- Nothing is sent and no row changes without approval.

## Write gate
Show the review in chat. Update list rows (`update-row`) only after the user approves.
