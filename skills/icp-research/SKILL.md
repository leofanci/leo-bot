# ICP Research

## Read first
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md`: problem, offer, the founder's reach
  and unfair advantages, evidence so far.
- The latest assessment's ICP hypotheses, the competitor memo and the evidence log.
- Missing intake: ask who buys today (or who the founder thinks will) and go on.

Use web search when it is available, for community sizes and where buyers gather.
Mark numbers you did not find as estimates.

## Method
1. **List 2 or 3 candidate segments.** Each one narrow: a role, a company type and
   size, a trigger moment ("clinics that just lost a receptionist").
2. **Score each segment from 1 to 5, with one line of reasoning per score:**
   - Pain: how badly and how often they hit the problem.
   - Reach: can this founder get in front of 10 of them this week?
   - Money: do they pay for a workaround today?
   - Size: enough of them to matter for the next 12 months.
   - Proof: evidence already in the log (paying customers, replies, interviews).
3. **Pick the beachhead:** narrow enough that 10 members would know each other or
   meet in the same places.
4. **Write the profile:** who decides, who uses, who pays; the trigger that makes
   them look for a fix; what they use today; the words they use for the problem.
5. **Where to find 10 this week:** specific communities, events, directories,
   search filters, newsletters and warm-network paths, tied to the founder's reach.
   No scraping of logged-in sites.
6. **Interview guide (8 to 10 questions)** about past behavior, not opinions of the
   idea: "Walk me through the last time you...", "What did you try?", "What did it
   cost you?". Never "Would you use...". Add the 2 or 3 answers that would confirm
   the segment and the 2 or 3 that would kill it.

## Options
The scored segments side by side: a small table, then one line each on the main
trade-off (easy to reach but low pain, high pain but hard to reach).

## Recommendation
The beachhead segment, why it wins on the scores, the biggest risk, and the
interview result that would make you switch to the runner-up.

## Before you reply
- Each segment is narrow enough to name 10 real members of it.
- Scores have reasons; facts, estimates and guesses are marked.
- No interview question asks for an opinion of the product.
- The "where to find them" list fits this founder, not any founder.

## Write gate
Propose the memo in chat (target `sec04`, `create-memo --type icp`). Commit only after the user approves.
