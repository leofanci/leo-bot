# Prospect List

## Read first
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md`: customer and offer.
- The ICP memo (segment, trigger, where they gather) and the channel memo.
- Existing lists in the workspace: never duplicate one; add to it.
- Missing ICP: ask for the segment in one line, or suggest `/icp-research` first.

## Method
1. **Write the filter:** role, company type and size, place, and the buying signal
   (a hiring post, a new location, a bad review of a rival, a tool they use).
2. **Pick sources** the owner may use: public directories, association member lists,
   public company pages, maps listings, event attendee pages, the owner's own network
   and customer referrals, or a licensed data tool the owner has connected. Never
   scrape logged-in sites or collect data a site's terms forbid.
3. **Set the fields:** name, company, role, source link, signal, fit score (1 to 3),
   status (new, contacted, replied, meeting, won, lost), next step, owner notes.
   Personal emails and phone numbers only from a public business page or the owner.
4. **Build a first batch of 10 to 25** that fit best, each with its source link and
   signal. Mark any field you inferred.
5. **Rank** by signal strength, then fit.

## Options
Two or three list shapes: a narrow list of warm leads (referrals, past contacts),
a list built on one buying signal, or a wider directory list. Trade-off: reply rate
against size and effort.

## Recommendation
The list shape, the first batch, and the reply rate you expect (an estimate) with
how many contacts that means for one meeting.

## Before you reply
- Every row has a source link; no row is made up.
- No private data from logged-in or paywalled sites the owner has no right to use.
- Nothing is sent: contact happens only after the owner approves each message.

## Write gate
Show the filter and the first rows in chat. Create the list (`create-list`, then `add-row`) only after the user approves.
