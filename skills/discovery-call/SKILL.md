# Discovery Call

## Read first
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md`: offer, price, customer.
- The ICP, positioning and pricing memos; any notes on this prospect (a list row,
  past messages the owner shares).
- Missing: ask who the call is with (role, company type) and what got them to agree
  to the call. Then go on.

## Method
1. **Set the call's goal:** learn whether they have the problem badly enough, and
   agree a next step. A call is not a pitch.
2. **Opening (2 minutes):** thank, agenda, time check, "is it fine if I ask some
   questions first?".
3. **Questions about the past, not the product** (8 to 10, pick for this prospect):
   - The situation: how they handle the job today, who is involved.
   - The pain: the last time it went wrong, what it cost (time, money, customers).
   - What they tried and why it fell short.
   - The trigger: why now, what changed.
   - The decision: who else decides, how they buy, budget owner, timing.
4. **Qualify:** score from 1 to 3 on pain, budget, authority, timing, fit. Write
   the facts heard, not impressions.
5. **Short demo or story only if the pain is confirmed:** show the one part that
   answers the pain they named, in their words.
6. **Ask for the next step:** a trial, a second call with the decision maker, a
   pilot at a price. A date, not "let's keep in touch".
7. **After the call:** a summary for the owner to send (draft only) and the
   evidence line to log (`add-evidence`), after approval.

## Options
Give two or three call plans when the prospect is unclear: discovery only, discovery
plus a short demo, or a pilot offer. One line each on when it fits.

## Recommendation
The plan for this call, the three questions that matter most, and the answer that
would mean "not a fit, stop here".

## Before you reply
- No question asks "would you use..." or "do you like...".
- The next step has a date and an owner.
- Any message to the prospect is a draft that waits for the owner.

## Write gate
Share the call plan in chat. Log what the call taught with `add-evidence` only after the user approves the text.
