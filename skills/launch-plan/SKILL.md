# Launch Plan

## Read first
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md`: stage, customers, evidence.
- The assessment (its pace call), the channel memo, the positioning memo.
- If the pace call says validate quietly, challenge the launch first: launching to
  find out if anyone wants it is usually backwards. Go on if the user confirms.
- Missing channel memo: plan only for channels the founder already uses.

## Method
1. **Set one goal.** One metric the launch must move and a number by a date
   (paid signups, booked demos, trial starts). Reach and likes are not goals.
2. **Write success and kill lines**, as for an experiment: what result means
   "keep pushing" and what result means "stop and rethink".
3. **Pick the launch type** (see Options).
4. **Sequence it:**
   - Before (1 to 3 weeks): the page, the proof (a quote, a number, a demo), a warm
     list of people who said they want it, seeding with a few friendly users.
   - Launch day: one main moment, the order of posts and messages, who answers
     replies and questions.
   - Week one: follow-ups to everyone who showed interest, a call with the first
     users, capture what they say in the evidence log.
5. **Channels:** only those in the channel memo or already working. One action per
   channel, with the asset it needs.
6. **Asset checklist:** each asset with an owner. Mark the ones the content skills
   can draft (`content-brief`, `copy-variants`).
7. **Flop plan:** what a quiet launch looks like in numbers, and what it still
   teaches (message, channel or offer).

## Options
- **Silent ship:** release to current users, learn from usage. Cheap, low risk, no reach.
- **Soft launch to one niche:** one community or segment. Good signal, limited reach.
- **Loud launch:** coordinated across channels on one day. Reach, but costly and one shot.
Give the trade-off of each for this stage and evidence.

## Recommendation
One launch type, the goal number, and the evidence that would change the choice.

## Reply shape
Goal and lines first, then the type, then the plan as a dated checklist. No "go
viral" plans and no channel the founder cannot run.

## Before you reply
- One goal metric with a number and a date.
- Every step has a date or a day and an owner.
- Anything sent or posted outside Leo Bot is a draft that waits for the user.
- The flop plan exists.

## Write gate
Propose the memo in chat (target `sec01`, `create-memo --type launch`). Commit only after the user approves.
