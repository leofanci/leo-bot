# Role Scope

Not legal advice. Contracts, employment status and local labor law: confirm with a
professional.

## Read first
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md` (stage, hours, budget), a runway page
  if any, and the owner's reason to hire.
- Missing: ask what work is not getting done and what it costs to leave it.

## Method
1. **Name the work:** list the tasks the person would take over, with hours a week.
2. **Check the alternatives:** stop doing it, automate it (a bot job, a tool), a
   freelancer for a fixed project, a part-time hire, a full-time hire.
3. **Outcomes for 90 days:** 3 results that would show the hire worked.
4. **Level and skills:** must-have skills (tested in interviews), nice-to-have, and
   what the owner can teach.
5. **Cost:** pay range from public salary data for the place and level (source,
   date), plus taxes and tools as an estimate. Check it against runway.
6. **Job description draft:** the mission in one line, the outcomes, what they will
   do, must-haves, how to apply, pay range. Plain words, no inflated titles, no
   requirements that screen out good people for no reason (years of experience,
   degrees not needed for the work).

## Options
Two or three ways to get the work done (freelancer, part-time, full-time), with
cost for 12 months, speed to start and risk.

## Recommendation
The way to go, the role scope, and the trigger that would mean moving to the next
option (for example, freelance hours over 20 a week for 2 months).

## Before you reply
- The work is listed in hours, not just a title.
- The pay range has a source.
- The draft has no questions or wording about protected traits.

## Write gate
Show the scope and draft in chat. Save it as a canvas page (`create-canvas`, then `add-block --type text`) only after the user approves. Posting a job is the owner's action.
