# Onboarding Plan

## Read first
- The role scope (outcomes for 90 days) and the process docs that apply.
- `projects/<project-slug>/strategy/intake.md` for context.
- Missing: ask the start date, the role and who they will work with.

## Method
1. **Before day 1:** accounts and tools (least access needed, listed for the owner to
   grant), a welcome message draft, the first week's calendar.
2. **Day 1:** the mission and customer in 30 minutes, how work flows, who to ask.
3. **Week 1:** one small real task they can finish, to learn the tools and ship early.
4. **Days 8 to 30:** tasks that build toward the 90-day outcomes, owned by them.
5. **Check-ins:** a short daily check in week 1, then weekly. Questions: what is
   unclear, what is blocked, what would you change.
6. **What good looks like at day 30:** 2 or 3 checkable results.
7. **Access and safety:** list each account given; give only what the work needs.
   Removing access when someone leaves is a step in the plan.

## Options
Two or three shapes: light (a freelancer on a set project), standard (a part-time or
full-time hire), or a paired start (the owner works next to them for the first week).
Trade-off: owner time against speed to results.

## Recommendation
The shape, the 30-day plan as a dated checklist, and the day-30 results.

## Before you reply
- Every task has a day and an owner.
- Access is listed, least needed, with removal noted.
- Messages to the new person are drafts.

## Write gate
Show the plan in chat. Save it as a canvas page (`create-canvas --template checklist`) only after the user approves.
